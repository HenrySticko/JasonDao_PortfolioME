AERODYNAMICS SOLIDWORKS CFRP LAYUP MANUFACTURING

Final Side Wing Assembly [With one endplate removed for clarity]: Multi-element profile for max downforce

Design Challenge: Cornering Stability

The main goal was to increase tire grip by generating significant downforce without creating excessive drag. This meant a lot of iterations in SolidWorks flow simulations to find the perfect angles of attack for the 3 elements of the wing. The overall concept to design to manufacturing process took about 3 months, or 1 quarter.

Airfoil Selection

Utilized E423 airfoil for low-speed airflow typical of SAE competition tracks and to maintain consistency with front and rear wings.

Composites Manufacturing

While we did not get to physically manufacture the side wings due to time constraints, I assisted other teams in manufacturing their wings using similar layup techniques, contributing a good couple days of my life to the workshop.

Vacuum Bagging

Executed precision wet layups and vacuum bagging to ensure optimal resin-to-fiber ratios.

Core Integration

Incorporated 3D-printed cores [as well as honeycomb core for endplates] to increase stiffness while maintaining a low mass.
